# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Project participation should be respectful, constructive, and focused on
helping people use and improve the software safely. Examples of positive
behavior include:

- giving actionable technical feedback
- respecting different levels of experience
- protecting user privacy and redacting sensitive data
- accepting maintainer decisions about safety and project scope

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publication of private
information, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

For a private conduct report, use the repository's **Report a vulnerability**
flow and begin the title with `Code of conduct report`. Do not include
credentials, user content, or unrelated personal information.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening,
or harmful.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other public spaces representing this project.
